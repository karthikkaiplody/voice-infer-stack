# Book summaries

## The Hound of the Baskervilles
A mystery by Arthur Conan Doyle, published in 1902. Sherlock Holmes and Dr Watson investigate the death of Sir Charles Baskerville and the legend of a giant ghostly hound that haunts his family on the moors of Devon. A classic detective story with a spooky atmosphere, and a good first Sherlock Holmes.
People often ask: what is The Hound of the Baskervilles about, tell me about the Hound of the Baskervilles, is it good, a summary.

## And Then There Were None
A mystery by Agatha Christie, published in 1939. Ten strangers are invited to a house on an island. One by one they die, following the lines of a nursery rhyme, and the survivors realise that the killer is one of them. Tense and quick to read, and one of the best-selling mysteries ever written.
People often ask: what is And Then There Were None about, tell me about And Then There Were None, a summary.

## The Thursday Murder Club
A cozy mystery by Richard Osman, published in 2020. Four friends in a retirement village meet every Thursday to look into old unsolved murders, until a real murder happens on their doorstep. Funny, warm and clever, and the first book in a series.
People often ask: what is The Thursday Murder Club about, tell me about the Thursday Murder Club, a funny mystery, a summary.

## The Martian
A science fiction novel by Andy Weir, published in 2011. Astronaut Mark Watney is left behind on Mars after a storm forces his crew to leave, and has to use his botany and engineering skills, and a lot of humor, to stay alive until a rescue can reach him. Fast, funny and full of problem solving.
People often ask: what is The Martian about, tell me about The Martian, the book about the astronaut on Mars, a summary.

## Dune
A science fiction epic by Frank Herbert, published in 1965. Young Paul Atreides and his family take charge of the desert planet Arrakis, the only source of a precious spice, and are betrayed by their enemies. A long, rich story about politics, religion and survival, best for readers who like big worlds.
People often ask: what is Dune about, tell me about Dune, the desert planet book, is Dune hard to read, a summary.

## Project Hail Mary
A science fiction novel by Andy Weir, published in 2021. A man wakes up alone on a spaceship with no memory, and slowly learns that he is a science teacher sent to stop a threat to the Sun and save the Earth. Fun, clever and moving, and easy to enjoy even if you do not usually read science fiction.
People often ask: what is Project Hail Mary about, tell me about Project Hail Mary, a summary.

## The Hobbit
A fantasy novel by J. R. R. Tolkien, published in 1937. Bilbo Baggins, a hobbit who loves his comfortable home, is swept into a quest with thirteen dwarves and the wizard Gandalf to win back treasure guarded by the dragon Smaug. Charming and adventurous, and good for readers young and old.
People often ask: what is The Hobbit about, tell me about The Hobbit, the book before Lord of the Rings, a summary.

## A Wizard of Earthsea
A fantasy novel by Ursula K. Le Guin, published in 1968. Ged, a gifted and proud young wizard, sets a dark shadow loose in the world and has to hunt it across the sea to put things right. A short, thoughtful book about power and growing up.
People often ask: what is A Wizard of Earthsea about, tell me about Earthsea, a short fantasy book, a summary.

## Piranesi
A fantasy mystery by Susanna Clarke, published in 2020. A man lives in a huge house of endless halls filled with statues and ocean tides, and keeps a journal of his world until clues show that things are not what they seem. Strange, quiet and beautiful.
People often ask: what is Piranesi about, tell me about Piranesi, a strange and unusual book, a summary.

## All the Light We Cannot See
A historical novel by Anthony Doerr, published in 2014. During the Second World War, a blind French girl named Marie-Laure and a young German radio expert named Werner live very different lives until their paths cross in the town of Saint-Malo. Beautifully written, with short chapters that are easy to pick up.
People often ask: what is All the Light We Cannot See about, tell me about that war novel, a summary.

## The Book Thief
A historical novel by Markus Zusak, published in 2005. Liesel, a girl living with a foster family in Nazi Germany, steals books and shares them with the people around her, including a Jewish man hidden in their basement. Narrated by Death, and moving without being hopeless.
People often ask: what is The Book Thief about, tell me about The Book Thief, a summary.

## Pride and Prejudice
A classic romance by Jane Austen, published in 1813. Elizabeth Bennet and the proud, wealthy Mr Darcy misjudge each other, and have to get past their first impressions and their families to find love. Sharp, witty and still very funny.
People often ask: what is Pride and Prejudice about, tell me about Pride and Prejudice, Elizabeth Bennet and Mr Darcy, a summary.

## Beach Read
A modern romance by Emily Henry, published in 2020. Two rival writers, one who writes love stories and one who writes serious literature, are neighbors for a summer and challenge each other to swap genres. Warm, funny and easy to read in a weekend.
People often ask: what is Beach Read about, tell me about Beach Read, a light summer read, a summary.

## Sapiens
Non-fiction by Yuval Noah Harari, published in 2011. A short history of humankind, from the first humans to today, explaining how shared ideas such as money, nations and laws let people work together on a huge scale. Big ideas in an easy style.
People often ask: what is Sapiens about, tell me about Sapiens, a book about human history, a summary.

## Educated
A memoir by Tara Westover, published in 2018. She grew up in a survivalist family in the mountains of Idaho, never went to school, and taught herself enough to go to university and eventually earn a doctorate. Gripping and honest, about family, education and finding your own mind.
People often ask: what is Educated about, tell me about Educated, a true story, a memoir, a summary.

## Atomic Habits
A self-help book by James Clear, published in 2018. It explains how tiny changes in your daily routine add up to big results, and gives a simple, practical system for building good habits and breaking bad ones. Clear, practical and quick to read.
People often ask: what is Atomic Habits about, tell me about Atomic Habits, a book about habits or self-improvement, a summary.

## Charlotte's Web
A children's classic by E. B. White, published in 1952. Wilbur the pig is about to be killed for meat until his friend Charlotte, a clever spider, spins words into her web to save him. A gentle story about friendship, good for reading aloud to children about six to ten.
People often ask: what is Charlotte's Web about, tell me about Charlotte's Web, a book for a young child, a bedtime story, a summary.

## Matilda
A children's novel by Roald Dahl, published in 1988. Matilda is a brilliant girl who loves reading and has neglectful parents. When she discovers a special power, she stands up to the terrible headmistress, Miss Trunchbull. Funny and clever, good for children about eight to twelve.
People often ask: what is Matilda about, tell me about Matilda, a funny book for my child, a summary.

## The Hunger Games
A young adult novel by Suzanne Collins, published in 2008. Katniss Everdeen volunteers to take her little sister's place in a televised contest where teenagers must fight to the death. Fast and suspenseful, best for teenagers and adults rather than young children.
People often ask: what is The Hunger Games about, tell me about The Hunger Games, a book for teenagers, a summary.
