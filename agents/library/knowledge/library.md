# Riverside Public Library

## Opening hours
The library is open 9am to 8pm Monday to Friday, 10am to 6pm on Saturday, and 12pm to 5pm on Sunday.
People often ask: what time do you open, when do you close, are you open today, weekend hours, Sunday hours.

## Borrowing and loans
Loans last three weeks. Books and other borrowed materials are due back after three weeks.
People often ask: how long can I borrow a book, how long can I keep it, when is it due.

## Renewals
Loans can be renewed twice online. To keep an item longer than three weeks, renew it online, up to two times.
People often ask: can I renew a book, how do I extend my loan.

## Fines and late fees
Fines are 10 cents a day for an overdue item, capped at 5 dollars.
People often ask: what are the late fees, how much is the fine, what happens if I return a book late.

## Library cards
A library card is free with proof of address.
People often ask: how do I get a library card, do I need to pay, what do I need to sign up.

## Study rooms
Study rooms can be booked two weeks ahead.
People often ask: can I reserve a room, how do I book a study room, how far ahead can I book.
